ABOUT

PROFILE

I typically move from vague intuitions or open problems to concrete action through iterative inquiry—gathering relevant knowledge and tools along the way and adapting them in practice.

HOW I WORK

Observe before judging. Understand a situation from the positions of those involved before forming conclusions.

Work with what is available. Start with existing resources, constraints, and relationships rather than waiting for ideal conditions.

Make complexity legible. Use visual, spatial, material, and analytical approaches to make complex ideas easier to understand and work with.

Recalibrate through feedback. Treat unexpected results and contradictions as information that can reshape the next step.
