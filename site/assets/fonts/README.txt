Practice Sans, Serif, and Mono are local WOFF conversions of Nimbus Sans,
Nimbus Roman, and Nimbus Mono PS from URW Base35 (URW Software).

Source: https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/urw-base35-fonts
The full upstream and distribution license text is in LICENSE.txt.
Conversion is reproducible with FontTools; see tools/build-materials.py.
These font files are served locally without a third-party font service.
