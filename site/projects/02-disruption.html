02 / INTERDEPENDENCE · OPERATIONAL SYSTEM

From Space to System

What sustains an independent system when responsibility, control, and conditions are unevenly distributed?

As the sole operator, with zero external funding, I transformed a previously unusable space into SUS CLINIC’s zero-waste and vintage operation. Physical adaptation, sourcing, material circulation, customer relationships, and day-to-day work had to function together.

I carried the budget and the consequences, but did not control every contractor, partnership, or platform the operation depended on. These five pages follow the decisions that made the space operable, distributed capacity, and exposed dependencies that were difficult to sustain.

My contribution: Spatial adaptation, material sourcing, brand identity, partnership coordination, staff recruitment, and independent operation.Xiamen, China · Renovation from February 2024 · Opened 1 March 2025
