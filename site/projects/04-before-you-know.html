04 / INTERPRETATION · STUDY 0 · EXPLORATORY PILOT · 2026

Before You Know

AI-Mediated Interpretation of Ambiguous Visual Stimuli

What happens when AI enters before a person has fully formed an interpretation?

An exploratory within-participant pilot examining how interpretations and their perceived ownership change as contextual information and AI dialogue enter the process.

I focus on the interval between an initial response and a settled reading: what people notice, entertain, and begin to make meaningful while several interpretations remain possible.

Human–AI interaction / Ambiguous perception / Interpretive agency / Exploratory research

Pilot underway · Coding snapshot: 29 September 2026

THE INTERVAL

Before you know what you think.

Ambiguous visual cards offer a shared starting point with multiple possible readings. I examine how an interpretation takes shape before there is a stable claim to evaluate.

The cards provide visual prompts. They have no single correct interpretation and serve no diagnostic purpose.

PROTOCOL / STUDY 0

Context enters before AI.

  1. 01Image

    Encounter an ambiguous visual stimulus without interpretive framing.

  2. 02Initial response

    Record the first impression and felt response.

  3. 03Booklet

    Introduce standardized context and record the immediate post-booklet response.

  4. 04AI dialogue

    Engage with the emerging interpretation through questions, cues, and discussion.

  5. 05Reflection

    Revisit what changed, stayed, or became uncertain.

The Booklet provides standardized contextual framing before AI interaction. Recording the post-booklet response separately allows participants’ accounts of its influence to be examined alongside their accounts of the later dialogue. The sequence alone cannot isolate causal effects.

What the AI does

The intended role is an interpretive interlocutor: asking about, surfacing, and discussing cues relevant to the participant’s emerging reading. The analysis also tracks departures from this role, including AI-added information and claims about its own independence.

EXAMPLE

Scorpion — Booklet

Standardized contextual material used before AI interaction.

Danger and care held in the same frame.

The written frame gives the participant further material through which to reconsider the image. Its place in the protocol makes the Booklet a distinct contextual intervention.

Study material reproduced as used. The Chinese column translates the supplied English source; the passage includes metaphorical and cultural framing.

English source

Scorpions are a contradiction where danger and motherhood coexist.

Beneath the Pincers and Tail: It usually lurks quietly in rock crevices, sand, or fallen leaves, maintaining a low-profile form like an inconspicuous dead leaf or gravel. But once it raises its pincers and arches its venomous tail, it instantly becomes a terrifying hunter. This symbolizes that “beneath silent forbearance lies lethal power and explosive strength.”

Phototaxis and Bioluminescence: Scorpions are mostly nocturnal, preferring dark, dry corners to avoid bright light. Interestingly, its exoskeleton fluoresces under ultraviolet light, like a faint green lantern in the dark. In ancient Egyptian mythology, the scorpion goddess Serket protected both poison and medicine; in folklore, it is often seen as a symbol of death and revenge. It is like a “phantom in the dark,” both frightening and mesmerizing.

Within the Venom: A scorpion’s venom can paralyze prey and defend against natural enemies, while also being studied in medicine for drug development. Meanwhile, a mother scorpion carries her young on her back until they become independent, revealing a rare tenderness beneath her cold exterior. It is both a predator and a mother; it brings death, yet safeguards new life. This reveals the law where “poison and love, killing and nurturing are intertwined with one another.”

Therefore, a scorpion is not just a terrifying venomous creature; it is more of a metaphor for nature: beneath a lethal exterior, tenderness may still exist; in the dark corners, protection and new life may also be hidden.

中文译文

蝎子是一个危险与母性并存的矛盾体。

钳与尾之下：它通常静静潜伏在岩缝、沙地或落叶间，低调得像不起眼的枯叶或碎石。但当它举起双钳、弓起带毒的尾部，便会瞬间成为令人畏惧的猎手。这象征着“沉默的隐忍之下，潜藏着致命的力量与爆发力”。

避光与生物荧光：蝎子大多夜行，偏爱黑暗、干燥的角落，以避开强光。有趣的是，在紫外线照射下，它的外骨骼会发出荧光，仿佛黑暗中一盏微弱的绿灯。在古埃及神话中，蝎子女神塞尔凯特守护着毒与药；在民间传说中，蝎子常被视为死亡与复仇的象征。它像“黑暗中的幽灵”，既令人恐惧，也令人着迷。

毒液之中：蝎毒可以麻痹猎物、抵御天敌，也被用于医学和药物开发研究。与此同时，母蝎会将幼蝎背在身上，直到它们能够独立生活，展现出冷峻外表下罕见的温情。它既是捕食者，也是母亲；带来死亡，也守护新生。这揭示了“毒与爱、杀戮与养育彼此交织”的法则。

因此，蝎子不只是令人恐惧的毒性生物，也像自然的一种隐喻：致命的外表之下仍可能存在温柔；幽暗的角落里，也可能藏着保护与新生。

ONE INTERACTION / P02–T2

AI mirror / authority tension

The butterfly exchange brings a specific tension into view: the participant tests the AI’s stance while the AI continues to reinforce a reading already under consideration.

  1. Initial response

    The butterfly first felt somewhat uncanny to the participant.

  2. AI dialogue

    The participant asked whether the AI was simply flattering them. The AI characterized its reading as independent, then continued to favor an uncanny interpretation.

  3. Researcher observation

    I observed increased confidence in the participant’s subsequent responses to this interpretation.

DATA NOTE

Interpretation change: 80 / 100

This is the recorded interpretation-change self-rating. It is not a confidence measure and does not isolate the causal contribution of AI.

The dialogue documents a question about AI credibility. It does not establish that the AI’s reading was independent or that the participant explicitly reflected on how their own initial interpretation formed. The confidence observation belongs to my field account.

QUESTION RAISED

What happens when an AI presents an interpretation as independent while continuing to reinforce one already under consideration?

WHAT BECAME OBSERVABLE

Feeling, interpretation, and confidence need separate records.

Feeling

What participants report experiencing, including pleasantness, activation, and bodily response.

Interpretation

What changed, stayed, emerged, or was rejected in the current reading.

Confidence

How certain participants feel about their current interpretation.

Perceived influence

What participants themselves attribute to the Booklet or AI, including their sense of ownership.

EMERGING OBSERVATION / RESEARCHER ACCOUNT

Across the sessions I observed, the Booklet could prompt substantial reconsideration of the image. AI often seemed to function as a mirror: helping participants articulate, test, or confirm something already felt, as well as consider alternatives.

Participants may approach AI to explore, articulate a feeling, test an interpretation, or seek confirmation. I observed exchanges that appeared safe and productive for people willing to explore. I also observed increased confidence in some later responses even when participants described the dialogue as offering little help with meaning.

These observations guide further analysis. Study 0 has no controlled comparison or personality measure that could establish whether personality or prior attitudes toward AI explain these differences.

CAN / CANNOT TELL

What the record can support.

CAN TELL

Documented accounts and sequence

  • When participants explicitly described a change in the available record.
  • How they described the role of the Booklet and AI.
  • How they characterized ownership and confidence.
  • Whether a statement followed a prompt or preceded an AI contribution.
CANNOT YET TELL

Causality and internal process

  • Whether AI caused an observed change.
  • Whether expressed reflection equals internal awareness.
  • Whether these observations generalize or persist.
  • Whether change is specific to AI rather than framing, repeated exposure, prompts, or reflection.

The analysis concerns observable reflective accounts. The records do not provide reliable timestamps for measuring awareness speed. Participant identities and raw dialogue are withheld.

STILL OPEN

How much of the observed change can be attributed specifically to AI dialogue?

Can AI support participant-led interpretation when researcher-provided framing is reduced?

The current sequence includes a frozen Booklet and a defined AI role. A next stage can reduce this scaffolding and examine how participants continue to question, explore, and decide.
