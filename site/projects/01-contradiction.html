01 / COORDINATION · CIVIC SYSTEM

Urban Micro-Ecosystem Governance

How can different actors build a shared basis for action within the same reality?

A municipal slope-safety project affected my family’s garage and fifteen other units. Residents had already begun discussing the dispute together, drawing on long-standing community knowledge and relationships to develop a shared position. I participated as a directly affected family member, without a formal leadership role.

The residents’ claims crossed property, safety, technical, administrative, and judicial processes. Within that collective effort, I compared records, contributed to discussions and factual communication, and followed how evidence and responsibility moved between institutions.

My contribution: Evidence organization, resident discussions, factual communication, and documentation as an affected family member. — Xiamen, China · 2022–2023

Civic coordination / Evidence / Negotiation / Recalibration

DOCUMENTED OUTCOME / NOVEMBER 2023

Two administrative decisions were revoked on 28 November 2023. The court permitted withdrawal of the related suit on 29 November 2023.

The documents establish the procedural outcome. My account describes one contribution within the residents’ collective effort.
