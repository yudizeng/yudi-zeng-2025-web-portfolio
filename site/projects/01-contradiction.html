01 / COORDINATION · CIVIC SYSTEM

Urban Micro-Ecosystem Governance

How can different actors build a shared basis for action within the same reality?

A municipal slope-safety project affected my family’s garage and fifteen other units. Families cleared spaces for the safety work. When removal and enforcement decisions followed, residents worked together across different records and procedures. I took part as a directly affected family member within that collective effort.

My contribution: Comparing and organizing records, taking part in resident discussions, and communicating relevant facts as the case moved through formal procedures. — Xiamen, China · 2022–2023
