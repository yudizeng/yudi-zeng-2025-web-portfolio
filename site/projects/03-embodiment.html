03 / TRANSLATION · EMBODIED PRACTICE

Translating the Invisible

How can narrative and bodily information be translated into material forms sensed through a moving body and a moving audience?

Across fashion and immersive theatre, I work with character, atmosphere, tension, intention, and movement—qualities that cannot be transferred directly from an idea to another person’s experience.

Observation and discussion inform material and costume decisions. Rehearsal then tests those decisions on moving bodies and in space. What becomes perceptible can change through use, performance, and encounter; the form is not final when it leaves the making process.

My contribution: Costume design, fabrication, and production for the principal characters, with visual guidance.PUPA Theatre collaboration · January–June 2024
